## What it does

It's an installation that rewards stillness. The longer you're still in front of it, the more the depth of the pattern emerges and exposes itself, drawing you in further. As soon as you move, or take yourself out of the present moment, it resets back to its earlier state, and you have to start over again.

There's a presence sensor hidden inside, so the piece knows when someone's there and whether they're moving or still.

## What you see

A disc of light behind frosted glass, about 30 cm across. I wanted the diffusion dreamy, soft enough that you almost don't notice the diodes. I left a gap around the disc because of how it looks from the side. Some light leaks out around the back onto the wall as a soft halo. That wasn't planned, and I liked it more than I expected.

The patterns are generated live and never repeat.

## Process

### Starting out

It started with an LED disk I bought, Adafruit's [DotStar disk](https://www.adafruit.com/product/2477), and an ESP32 on my desk. From September 25 I prototyped visuals on it.

### The mirror, and the idea

I picked up a round mirror at a flea market, and for a while the plan was to mount the disc on it and let it rotate. On October 2 I dropped that. Presence was the more interesting idea, and rotation was one more thing that could break. When the radar first worked on the bench, I couldn't tell how it knew I was there.

### Test prints

The enclosure is 3D printed, and I printed, sanded, soldered and assembled all of it by hand. The soldering alone took a full day. The clear wall is printed in four quarters, because that's what fits on the printer. Straight off the printer every LED turned into a vertical streak, and sanding fixed it.

### The mirror cracks

The piece was meant to stand on the mirror. The drill went through the steel pan easily, then stalled on the glass. I pushed, and it cracked as it went through. I should have been more patient. So it went up without the mirror, clamped straight to the back box, and the halo on the wall is what you get instead.

## Parts

- 255 LEDs in concentric rings on a 242 mm disk
- ESP32-S3, patterns generated live
- LD2410C 24 GHz presence radar, hidden under the glass
- Clear PLA wall and spider, frosted glass, printed back box
- Battery inside, one switch, Wi-Fi updates
- About 30 cm across, 10.5 cm off the wall
